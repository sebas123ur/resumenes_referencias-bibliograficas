# Privacy Policy for Summary & Reference Assistant

**Effective date:** August 26, 2026

Summary & Reference Assistant ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension handles your information.

## Information Collection and Use
The extension is designed to help users generate instant summaries and academic references from web pages. 
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, user accounts, or browsing history.
- **On-Demand Processing:** The extension only reads the text content, title, and URL of the active tab when you explicitly click the extension and request a summary or citation.

## AI Processing & Third-Party APIs
- To generate summaries and citations, the extracted page text and metadata are securely sent on-demand to a third-party AI provider (such as Groq). 
- This data is transmitted exclusively to fulfill your immediate request and return the generated output. It is never permanently stored, used for tracking, or used by the provider to train AI models.

## Permissions Used
- **activeTab & scripting:** Used strictly on-demand to extract text content from the current page when triggered by the user. No background tracking or persistent script injection is performed.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
